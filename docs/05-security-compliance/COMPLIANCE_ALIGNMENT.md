# Compliance Alignment

> AI-Mail · Content · Pre-Alpha

AI-Mail is cataloged as a Content application focused on email and messaging operations.

> **Baseline notice:** This document defines the expected operating standard. It does not claim that a control, integration, model, or certification is already implemented; verify the code, configuration, and production evidence before release.

## Position

No certification or regulatory scope is asserted. Privacy, contractual, accessibility, and security obligations still require review before release. This document is a control-mapping workspace, not a certification, legal opinion, registration, or representation of compliance.
